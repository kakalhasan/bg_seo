## 6. Local Reach Gaps

**One locality page added this run: Shanti Nagar (Mira Road store).**

- **Resolved: Shanti Nagar (Mira Road) — added.** Open since 2026-10-08 on overlap-with-Naya-Nagar concerns. This run's WebSearch found an MBMC (Mira Bhayandar Municipal Corporation) municipal-map source describing Shanti Nagar with its own numbered sectors (Sector 1 through 11) — i.e., a genuinely distinct, officially-recognized part of Mira Road East, administratively separate from Naya Nagar, not merely the same station-adjacent ground under a second name (the earlier runs' worry). Two independent coordinate points agreed closely: Shanti Shopping Centre (opposite Mira Road station, ≈19.280230, 72.857170) and a Shanti Nagar Sector 2 building listing (≈19.277098, 72.859100) gave straight-line distances of 2.15 km and 2.07 km respectively from the store (19.2843751, 72.8772246) — road-est (×1.3) 2.7–2.8 km, both comfortably inside the 10 km radius and mutually consistent. Used 2.7 km / "9-11" minutes, interpolated from the existing Mira Road ratio pattern (Naya Nagar 2.1km→8-10min, Bhayandar West 3.4km→10-12min). Wrote a distinct blurb anchored on the Shanti Shopping Centre landmark and the sector-numbering fact, framed around station commuters — deliberately different framing from Naya Nagar's existing blurb (visor-pin repair, short detour).
- **Kanakia — dropped from further pursuit, not logged as a candidate.** WebSearch this run found "Off Kanakia Road, Mira Road East, Near Gaurav City" in a business listing — Gaurav City/Gaurav Sankalp is the store's own landmark. This places Kanakia essentially on top of the store's own immediate neighborhood rather than a distinct nearby locality worth a separate page. Not re-logging this one; closing it out as a dead end.
- **Nerul East (Navi Mumbai) — checked, not pursued.** WebSearch found Nerul East sits only ~1-3 km from Seawoods/the store's own address (Seawoods is Nerul's southern sub-node, store is in "Seawoods, West, Nerul"). Too close to be a distinct "nearby locality" rather than an extension of the store's own immediate area — same reasoning as Kanakia above. Not added, not logged as a candidate (there's nothing here a future run could resolve differently).
- Checked whether anything new is missing for the other two comparatively thin stores (Mira Road: now 6 localities, Navi Mumbai: 6, vs 13-14 for the other three) — no further viable candidate surfaced for Navi Mumbai this run; prior rejections (Kasarvadavali, Aarey Colony, Airoli, Ulwe, Mira Road West, Koparkhairane, Ghansoli, Uttan, Dahisar West, Balkum) were not re-examined, their reasoning stands.
