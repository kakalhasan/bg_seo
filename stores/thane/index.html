Frequently asked

Does the Thane store stock ISI and DOT certified helmets?

Yes. Every helmet sold is ISI marked as required by Indian law, with ECE and DOT certified options from LS2 and other brands across full-face, modular and open-face styles.

Can I try on jackets, gloves and boots before buying?

Yes — walk in and test-fit anything in-store. Sizing isn't identical across brands, so we fit you at the counter instead of you guessing a size online.
